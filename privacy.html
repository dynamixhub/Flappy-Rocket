Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026 · Flappy Rocket by Dynamix Studio

This Privacy Policy explains what data Flappy Rocket collects and how it's used. We built this game to be simple and respectful of your privacy — there are no accounts, no forms, and nothing to sign up for.

Information stored on your device

Flappy Rocket stores two small values locally in your browser using localStorage, so they stay on your device and are never sent to us:

  • Your best score, so it can be shown to you on future visits
  • Your sound on/off preference

You can clear this at any time via your browser's site settings.

Third-party advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense and other third-party ad networks. These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and opt out of personalized advertising at Google's Ads Policy page.

What we don't collect

We don't collect names, emails, precise location, or any other personal information directly. We don't require sign-in, and there's no server-side account tied to your play data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the game or the ad networks used. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Dynamix Studio.
