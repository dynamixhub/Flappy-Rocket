Flappy Rocket

Flappy Rocket is a quick-reflex space dodger: pilot a rocket through a scrolling field of energy pylons without clipping your wings. One tap, one rule — survive as long as you can and chase your best distance.

Made by

Dynamix Studio — this game was designed and built by Dynamix Studio.

How to play

Tap the screen, click, or press Space / the Up arrow to thrust. Gravity pulls your rocket down constantly, so timing each thrust is what keeps you threading the gaps. The field gets tighter and faster the longer you survive.
